CONCURRENT EDITING: DESIGN STILL NEEDS LOCAL VERIFICATION
Excel coauthoring on OneDrive/SharePoint does not make direct Python XLSX file
replacement a safe coauthoring operation. openpyxl rewrites the file. Reloading first,
mtime checks, flock and atomic replace cannot protect an edit still on another device.
Investigate updating through Excel on the Mac while the workbook participates in
coauthoring. Verify automation access, protected-cell updates/new-row insertion and
preservation of Comments in a second editing session. This is an unverified candidate,
not a proven implementation. Do not claim safe concurrent comments based only on unit
tests or a successful local save.
If that approach cannot meet the requirement, explain the finding before substituting
Microsoft Graph or separate comments files; the user has not selected those alternatives.
Keep a durable local event journal/outbox and retry shared publication by event_id
without duplicated rows or calls. Distinguish activity output from process/diagnostic logs.
