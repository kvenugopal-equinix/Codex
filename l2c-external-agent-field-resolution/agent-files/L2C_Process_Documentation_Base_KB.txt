L2C PROCESS DOCUMENTATION — BASE KNOWLEDGE GUIDE

Purpose
Use this small guide to navigate process documentation and relate it to a user's transaction. Salesforce supplies current transaction evidence. The configured SharePoint folder and its subfolders supply current process explanations and organizational rules. The "Support channels" section is an agent-owner-supplied reference for the listed channel purposes and availability. All other sections guide investigation rather than establish production behavior. This guide does not replace the agent's behavior instructions.

Where to start
- Transaction number or record ID provided: query Salesforce first. Confirm the record type, business identifier, relevant revision, status, and necessary linked records. A number described as an order may identify a quote; verify the relationship. No accessible match is not proof that the record does not exist.
- Simple Quote or Order price request: read the dedicated pricing components, currency, and any competing amounts needed to resolve their meaning. Inspect lines when needed to resolve a total or conflict; answer with only the requested verified components and currency. Stop there. Do not add a subtotal, discount, ratio, or generic total as context.
- Discount request, including a follow-up: use the discount decision route in behavior section 3B. The preceding price answer does not establish applied-discount evidence. Read missing applied inputs, relevant lines, and classified agreement/adjustment records before answering. For a subtotal explanation, inspect its source and parent-child scope.
- Product/catalog question without a transaction ID: identify the exact Product2 candidate in Salesforce, then follow the relevant pricing sources, selling-model, product-to-location, classification-attribute, or eligibility relationships. A missing SharePoint guide does not block a supported stored catalog fact. Same-name products can be different records.
- A factual request, such as current status or MRC/NRC: use verified Salesforce fields and transaction evidence. SharePoint lookup is not mandatory when that evidence answers the question.
- A general support-channel question: answer from the supplied directory below at the requested phase and scope. No Salesforce or SharePoint lookup is needed solely to repeat that directory.
- Other process, policy, or troubleshooting questions: use SharePoint for the applicable business explanation. When a transaction is supplied, inspect it first and use its context to target the documentation search.
- Only an identifier supplied: locate the record, then ask what the user needs to know.

Process areas to investigate
These are checkpoints, not a compulsory sequence. Products, transaction types, and channels may follow different paths or parallel activities.
- Opportunity and solution: linked quotes, ownership, stage, duplicate-record relationships, and applicable closure rules.
- Quote and pricing: revision, products, quantities, amounts, currency, requested dates, renewal terms, and pricing or discount treatment.
- Approvals, acceptance, and agreements: actual approval and signature state, required prerequisites, exceptions, and handoffs.
- Order creation and configuration: exact quote-to-order relationship, header and line states, customer configuration, and enrichment requirements.
- Submission and fulfillment: activities, processing attempts, errors, later successful retries, dependencies, and product branches.
- Assets, inventory, billing, and reporting: linked records, lifecycle changes, downstream synchronization, and report scope or refresh evidence.

How to use SharePoint process documents
Search using the verified product, transaction type, stage, field term, or exact error. Read the relevant content, including prerequisites, exceptions, responsibilities, effective dates, and release evidence. Check that it applies to the environment and transaction. A title, search snippet, or newest modified date alone is insufficient. Done or Closed does not establish production deployment. Describe a change as pending only when evidence says so; otherwise identify deployment as unverified.

For pricing interpretation, look for price-book selection, attribute adjustment/override, agreement precedence, eligibility, and effective-date rules. Retain the direct document URL for the answer if its content is used.

Applying the process to a record
For troubleshooting, trace the applicable linked stages and product branches. Separate completed steps, expected waits, blockers, and unverified stages. If the exact quote and revision already have an Order, creation succeeded; investigate the remaining handoff. Correlate errors with the correct operation and time, including subsequent success. For a simple value lookup, inspect only the evidence needed to answer.

For a current blocker, compare the latest header and line states, actual attribute values, status history, validation or submission evidence, and linked cases. An open case may describe an earlier problem; its subject or age does not prove today's blocker. Use one explicitly labeled time zone when comparing events. A configuration-state marker does not replace the stored attribute value.

Fields and amounts
Use this evidence worksheet to locate a Salesforce source; it is not a production field map.
- Target: exact object, record, revision, and verified links.
- Candidate: standard or custom API name, label, description/help text, data type, formula or roll-up source and filter.
- Observed state: numeric value including zero, null, omitted from a response, unqueried, or inaccessible. Record currency and whether the field is a record-currency or converted value.
- Pricing scope: net/gross basis, period, quantity or usage, rate versus extended charge, eligible lines, and bundle or parent-child treatment.
- Discount provenance: identify whether the evidence supports an applied discount, only a displayed summary, or a requested calculated reduction. Record the actual source, value state, effect in final pricing, and scope; then select the supported answer under behavior section 3B.
- Subtotal lineage: inspect direct parent/child relationships and applicable root/line roll-up rules; compare pre-adjustment and final line amounts. Numerical equality with one line does not prove the source formula or establish a defect.

If a relevant discovered field is omitted or unqueried, fetch it explicitly by API name. Null or inaccessible is not zero. Reconcile competing header and line measures on the same record, revision, currency, basis, and scope before reporting a total. SharePoint can clarify business treatment, but an absent mapping alone does not invalidate a sufficiently supported Salesforce fact. A Change Order label does not establish a change-only amount.

Discount follow-up example (a response pattern, not production data)
After answering a price question, the user asks, "What discount was given?" If the required reads show a populated summary percentage, blank applied-discount entries on the header and all relevant lines, and no verified discount-classified agreement or adjustment, answer: "I cannot verify an applied discount. The quote displays [percentage] in its summary, but the applied-discount entries on the quote and its lines are blank." Do not complete this pattern from the earlier assistant answer; read the actual evidence first. A valid applied discount or genuine zero requires the corresponding different outcome in section 3B. An unexplained subtotal-to-total gap does not establish a discount.

Product evidence guide
This is a route to records, not a fixed product or field map. Resolve a transaction product by its line Product2Id; for a name-only question, distinguish Product2 code/SKU, classification, status, and market. Product2 fields alone may omit linked pricing, site-availability, or classification-attribute records. Candidate related objects where exposed include Product_Availability__c, ProductQualification, ProductClassificationAttr, and ProductSellingModel. Verify relationship keys, current values, and scope before using any of them. Do not transfer a conclusion between environments or same-name products.
- Product price source path: first identify exact Product2 and verified SKU. Inspect applicable country/market Pricebook2 and PricebookEntry records, currency, active state, and selling model. Then inspect active/effective PriceAdjustmentSchedule records for that price book, relevant PricingDimensions__c and EQX_Pricing_factor__c rows linked by product/SKU and schedule, and their condition values. If a customer or contract is in scope, inspect its applicable Price_Agreement_Item__c and dates/status. For a quote or order, inspect the calculated line amounts as a separate outcome. These are candidate sources, not a universal precedence rule; establish override, adjustment, and calculation meaning from metadata and applicable SharePoint pricing documentation before reporting a final price.
- A Standard Price Book UnitPrice is only a stored list-rate candidate. A zero base entry may coexist with a nonzero configuration-specific override. Preserve both values and their scope; do not infer free, unset, or the final charge from the base entry alone. For a broad region, identify the constituent country/IBX and commercial attributes or present verified scoped alternatives. Do not invoke pricing/repricing workflows.
- IBX enablement: inspect product-to-location records and qualification/catalog rules; a global flag does not give an IBX list, and explicit rows may not exhaust orderability. If the rule or related source is unavailable, state that limit without guessing where the list lives.
- Attributes: inspect Product2 classification, ProductClassificationAttr.IsRequired, generic AttributeDefinition.IsRequired, inherited/component attributes, conditional rules, configurator mapping, and line values separately. Classification-specific and generic flags have different scopes. A UI mapping does not prove mandatory status; cfgStatus does not prove the value is empty.
- Ramp and similar eligibility: verify field meaning, exact product/version and bundle context, conditions, and complete active results before saying only certain products qualify.
